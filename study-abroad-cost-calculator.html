      How the total is calculated
      • Monthly cost = rent + living costs + any other expenses you add that recur (yearly items such as trips are spread evenly per month).
      • Total for your stay = monthly cost × months + one-time costs you added (flights at the start and end, visa fees, setup).
      • Living costs cover food, local transport, phone and bills, and personal essentials. Lean, Standard and Comfortable set how much you eat out, travel by taxi and spend on essentials; Fine-tune lets you set each one.
      • A refundable deposit is not a cost, so it is never in the total. It appears only in cash needed to get started.
