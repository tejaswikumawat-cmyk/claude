      How the total is calculated
      • Weekly or monthly cost: the UK, Australia and New Zealand let rooms by the week, so every figure there is shown per week; other countries are shown per month. It is rent + living costs + any other expenses you add that recur (yearly items such as trips are spread evenly).
      • Total for your stay = weekly (or monthly) cost × weeks (or months) of stay + one-time costs you added (flights, visa fees). A year is 52 weeks.
      • Living costs cover food, local transport, phone and bills, and personal essentials. Lean, Standard and Comfortable set how much you eat out, travel by taxi and spend on essentials; Fine-tune lets you set each one.
