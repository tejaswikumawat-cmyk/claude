    How the total is calculated
    • Total = monthly running cost × months of stay + one-time costs. The refundable deposit is not a cost and is excluded from the total.
    • Monthly running cost includes yearly items (trips, annual memberships) spread evenly per month, plus your contingency buffer.
    • One-time costs are visa and residence fees, insurance and levies paid upfront, flights at the start and end of your stay, a starter household kit and winter clothing where needed.
    • Cash needed to get started = one-time costs + refundable deposit + your first month of running costs.
    • Meals: the plan always adds up to 21 meals a week; home cooking covers every meal you do not eat out.
    • Likely range widens with uncertainty: ±5% on DB-backed and official items, ±15% on sourced estimates, ±30% on estimates.
    Sources and exchange rates
    Estimates for planning only. Confirm visa, insurance and fee amounts on official government sites before paying. Tuition is not included.
